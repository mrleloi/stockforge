# KOL Seed List

> Populate with 10-20 Vietnamese finance KOLs you want to start tracking.
> This becomes input to BC-6 (spec 002).
> Data here seeds the calibration database — outcomes collected over 6-12 months will score each.

## Purpose

- Define initial KOL universe for Phase 2 ingestion
- Capture your pre-calibration gut estimates (later compared to data-driven scores)
- Reveal your own biases — KOLs you thought were good might not be, vice versa

## Rules

- Public channels only (BR-1)
- No accusations — "style" classification is descriptive, not accusatory
- "Gut credibility" is your estimate BEFORE data — will be contradicted by calibration, and that's useful

## Template

```markdown
## {KOL Name or Channel Name}

- **Primary platform**: YouTube | Facebook | Telegram | Podcast | Blog
- **URL**: {full URL}
- **Est audience**: {subscriber/follower count}
- **Style** (descriptive): FUNDAMENTAL | TECHNICAL | NARRATIVE | MIXED
- **Sectors covered**: {list}
- **Gut credibility** (before calibration): 1-10
- **Strengths** (your observation): {what they do well}
- **Weaknesses** (your observation): {where they miss}
- **Known biases** (if any): {e.g., "always bullish", "hyper-bearish", "promotes specific brokerage"}
- **Track record highlights**: {specific calls that stuck in memory}
- **Track record lowlights**: {specific calls that went wrong}
- **Watch frequency recommendation**: daily | weekly | monthly
```

## Candidates to Consider (common VN finance KOLs — do your own research)

Note: listing names here doesn't endorse. You research and decide which to track.
Remove this section after your list is populated with actual entries above.

Common categories to explore:
- **Broker-affiliated KOLs** (analysts with brokerage background)
- **Independent YouTubers** (full-time content creators)
- **Podcast hosts** (long-form analysis)
- **Substack/blog writers** (written analysis)
- **Former fund managers** (highest quality signal usually)
- **Community moderators** (forum-famous users)

Do your own search. Look for:
- Consistent output (>1 video/post per week)
- Actual stock picks (not just market commentary)
- At least 6 months of history to evaluate
- Vietnamese language primary (English fine secondary)
