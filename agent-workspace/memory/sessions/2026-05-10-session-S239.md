## Lessons Learned

**L-S239-1** (NEW): Oversold technical signals in sparse information environment are unactionable. Mechanical signals (RSI <10) should not be presented as bull cases without supporting fundamental data, confirmed catalyst, or peer comparison. Mean-reversion trades require anchor in both technicals AND fundamentals.

**L-S239-2** (pattern affirmation): Zero-news periods are inherent constraint for narrative-driven markets like Vietnam. Absence of KOL discussion, news coverage, or analyst mentions is itself a strong headwind signal, not neutral backdrop.

**L-S239-3** (architecture insight): Single-ticker SharedContext must include peer layer (minimum 1 comparable stock + sector median) to enable valuation contextualization. PE metric alone is insufficient.
