## Lessons learned (1 candidate; L-S15-1)

### L-S15-1: AskUserQuestion multi-batch packing — foundational + topical coherence beats equal batch sizes

**Rule**: When firing AskUserQuestion multi-batch (>4 questions), pack most-foundational + topically-coherent items into Batch 1 at 4-question max (the Anthropic-designed limit); subsequent batches absorb remainder per topical coherence. Don't force equal sizes (e.g., 3+3+3 for 9 questions). Reason: user mental-model coherence per batch matters more than uniform pacing — a 4-question governance batch followed by a 2-question drift batch reads more naturally than three muddled 3-question batches.

**Anti-example**: 3+3+3 split forces splitting Q-E3 (promo priority) and Q-E2 (promo frequency) — sister concerns — across batches OR moving Q-D3 (memory-tiers) to Batch 2 where it's topically isolated.

**Correct example**: 4+3+2 — Batch 1 governance defaults (Q-E3+E2+B2+D3 all decision-discipline.md content); Batch 2 autonomous-protocol inputs (Q-C2+C3+2.1 all autonomous-protocol.md content); Batch 3 drift detection+recovery (Q-E1+E4).

**Severity**: low (workflow detail; not correctness).

**Auto-detect**: no (design-time judgment).

**Promotion candidate**: amend `grill-maximization/SKILL.md` § Multi-batch composition or `references/multi-batch-pattern.md` with this rule. Track 7 IMPL (S16) candidate.
